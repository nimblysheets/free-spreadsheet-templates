## Start here

1. Download the workbook that matches your language and currency display.
2. Open it in Excel, or upload the `.xlsx` file to Google Drive and choose **Open with Google Sheets**.
3. Read the first instruction tab.
4. Replace the fictional example rows and review the editable categories and assumptions.
5. Check formulas and outputs against your own source records before relying on them.

The EUR, USD, MXN and BRL labels describe visible number formats. The files do not convert currencies, retrieve exchange rates, connect to accounts or platforms, or verify external information.
